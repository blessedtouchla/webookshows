Mr. Capone-E

Born in Pakistan and raised in West Covina, Mr. Capone-E built his career independently through his own label, Hi Power Entertainment, which he founded in 2000.

His oldies-driven albums and collaborations with Nate Dogg, Twista and Snoop Dogg made him a fixture of the SoCal scene.

He brings a loyal, multi-generational crowd.

Lil Rob

Lil Rob was born in San Diego and raised in the La Colonia de Eden Gardens neighborhood of Solana Beach.

His 2005 single "Summer Nights" became a Billboard Hot 100 hit and a warm-weather anthem that crowds still sing word for word.

His catalog runs from early-'90s street records to smooth, oldies-flavored singles.
