Brockett Parsons

Brockett Parsons has been Lady Gaga's keyboard player since 2010, across world tours, network TV and the Super Bowl LI halftime show.

A Berklee-trained player rooted in jazz, R&B and hip-hop, he is also a co-inventor of the PianoArc, a circular 360-degree keyboard that turns a solo set into a spectacle.

He is a polished, versatile pick for hotel lounges, corporate events and upscale after-parties.
