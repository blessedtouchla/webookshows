Rappin' 4-Tay

Rappin' 4-Tay grew up in San Francisco's Fillmore District and got his first break on a Too Short album straight out of high school.

His 1994 album Don't Fight the Feelin' produced "Playaz Club" and "I'll Be Around," both Billboard Hot 100 hits, and he later appeared on 2Pac's "Only God Can Judge Me."

He has also headlined the main stage at Seattle Hempfest (2013).

Suga Free

Suga Free came out of Pomona with a rapid, conversational delivery that is instantly recognizable.

His 1997 debut Street Gospel was produced by DJ Quik and introduced "If U Stay Ready," and he has since appeared alongside Snoop Dogg, Xzibit, E-40 and Kokane.

On stage he is equal parts storyteller and showman.
