Baby Bash

Baby Bash is a Vallejo-born rapper and singer who came up through Houston's Dope House Records before breaking through nationally.

"Suga Suga" with Frankie J reached the Billboard Hot 100 top ten in 2003, followed by hits like "Baby, I'm Back" with Akon and "Cyclone" with T-Pain.

His radio-friendly catalog is ideal for casinos, hotel pool parties and summer festivals.
