RBL Posse

RBL Posse came out of San Francisco's Hunters Point in the early '90s, and their self-produced debut single "Don't Give Me No Bammer" made the Billboard rap singles chart.

Founding member Black C carries the group forward today and keeps releasing new RBL Posse and solo material.

The set is raw, regional and built for fans of classic Bay Area street rap.
