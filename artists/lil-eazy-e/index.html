Lil Eazy-E

Lil Eazy-E is the Compton-born son of N.W.A founder Eazy-E, and he carries the family legacy on stage.

He is president of Rich & Ruthless Records and co-founded Rich & Ruthless Cannabis with his brother.

His show leans on the West Coast classics that built Compton's name, which makes it a strong draw for throwback nights and hemp events.
