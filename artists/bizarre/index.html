Bizarre

Detroit's Bizarre is best known as a member of D12, the crew whose debut Devil's Night opened at No. 1 on the Billboard 200.

He sharpened his skills at the rap battles in Detroit's Saint Andrew's Hall, and his solo debut single "Rockstar" featured Eminem.

His over-the-top humor and D12-era hits make for an unpredictable, high-energy set.
