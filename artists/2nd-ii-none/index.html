2nd II None

Compton cousins KK and Gangsta D formed 2nd II None alongside their childhood friend DJ Quik, who produced most of their 1991 self-titled debut.

"Be True to Yourself" and "If You Want It" brought them national attention, and "Up 'N Da Club" later turned up on The Sopranos.

It is a smooth, funk-laced set for classic West Coast crowds.
