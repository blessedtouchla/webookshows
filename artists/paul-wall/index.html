Paul Wall

Paul Wall is a Houston rapper and DJ who came up through Swishahouse before signing with Atlantic.

His 2005 album The Peoples Champ debuted at No. 1 on the Billboard 200, and his feature on Nelly's "Grillz" hit No. 1 on the Hot 100 and earned a Grammy nomination.

He is a crowd-friendly headliner for festivals, casinos and car-culture events.
