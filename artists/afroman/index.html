Afroman

Afroman turned a homemade song into a worldwide sing-along: "Because I Got High" spread from his own independent releases to a 2002 Grammy nomination for Best Rap Solo Performance.

He first performed in church playing drums and guitar, and his show still has that loose, musical, crowd-first sense of humor.

He has stayed independent and prolific, and it plays as well on a festival field as it does in a casino showroom.
