Who we book for

  • Casino showrooms

  • Festival stages

    Main-stage and support slots, from city festivals to multi-day lineups.

  • Clubs & theaters

    Club and theater bookings with routing-friendly packages and double bills.

    Polished sets and appearances for rooftops, pool parties and lounges.

How booking works

  1. Send an inquiry

    Date, city, venue, capacity and the artists you have in mind.

  2. Get availability

    We check the date with the artist's team and come back with availability and a quote for that date.

  3. Confirm the offer

    Terms, rider and logistics are confirmed in writing before anything is announced.
